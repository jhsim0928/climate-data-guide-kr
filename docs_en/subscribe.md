# Notice alerts

Get an email when a new notice — end of production, service migration, quality issue — is posted. **Choose only the datasets and severity levels you care about.**

!!! tip "The confirmation email may land in spam"
    If the confirmation email is not in your inbox, please check your **spam folder**. If it is there, mark it as **Not spam** so that future alerts reach your inbox. Adding `data4researchers@gmail.com` to your contacts also helps.

<!-- SUBSCRIBE_FORM -->

## How alerts work

- Notices are posted only after the maintainer has checked the official source; unconfirmed news is never sent.
- New notices are bundled into one email once a day (around 09:00 KST).
- After signing up you will receive a confirmation email. Your subscription is active only after you click **Confirm subscription**. If you cannot find it, check your spam folder and mark it as "Not spam".
- Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can unsubscribe at any time.
- Severity levels are the same as on the [Notices](notices.md) page: **Critical · Warning · Info**.
