# Privacy policy

The Korean Climate Data Navigator (the "site") collects only the minimum information needed to send notice alerts and to receive dataset suggestions and error reports.

## 1. What we collect and why

| Information | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Email address | Sending the confirmation email and notice alerts for the datasets you chose |
| Selected datasets and severity levels | Sending only the notices you asked for |
| Time of sign-up and confirmation | Record of consent |
| Language of the page you signed up on | Sending emails in that language |
| Type of affiliation, position and research area (optional) | Aggregate statistics on who uses the Navigator, to choose datasets and improve the site; never published individually or used for anything else |
| Dataset suggestion; name, affiliation, contact (optional) | Reviewing the suggestion, replying, crediting contributors if you wish |
| Error report, page address, reply email (optional) | Correcting the site, replying |
| Feedback about the site, reply email (optional) | Improving the site, replying |

There is no account or login, and nothing else is collected.

## 2. How long we keep it

- Alert subscriptions: deleted **without delay** when you unsubscribe. Sign-ups that are not confirmed via the email link are deleted within 30 days.
- Suggestions, error reports and feedback: kept for review and replies; deleted without delay on request.
- If the service ends, all information is deleted.

## 3. Sharing with third parties

We do not share your information with any other person or organisation.

## 4. Service providers

| Provider | Task |
|---|---|
| Google LLC (Gmail, Google Sheets, Google Apps Script) | Storing subscriptions, suggestions, error reports and feedback; sending confirmation and alert emails |
| GoatCounter | Cookie-free visitor statistics (no personally identifiable information stored) |

Google's servers are located outside Korea, so submitted information may be stored abroad (e.g. in the United States). The transfer happens when you submit a form, over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

## 5. Unsubscribing and deletion

Click the **unsubscribe** link at the bottom of any alert email; your subscription is removed immediately. You can also ask via the contact address below.

## 6. Visitor statistics

Visits are counted with a cookie-free tool (GoatCounter). Only aggregate statistics such as page views, approximate country and browser type are kept; nothing that identifies you personally.

## 7. Contact

- Maintainer: Korean Climate Data Navigator maintainer

Effective date: 12 October 2026. The Korean version of this policy is the governing text.
