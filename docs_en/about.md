# About & citation

## Why this site exists

Long model runs and multi-decade analyses are easily derailed when a reanalysis suddenly stops updating or a download service changes. Those announcements are scattered across agency forums, news pages and mailing lists, and they are easy to miss.

This site brings three things together:

1. **Where and how to get the data** — official providers, whether an account is needed, example code
2. **What to put in your paper** — dataset DOIs, reference papers, acknowledgement wording
3. **Whether it is usable right now** — end of updates, service migrations, quality issues, successor plans

## Principles

- Every fact is checked against the **official source**, and every notice links to the original announcement.
- We only publish what we could confirm on official pages, and we correct entries when things change.
- Dataset names, variable names, DOIs and code are kept exactly as the providers write them.
- We do not store or redistribute any data.
