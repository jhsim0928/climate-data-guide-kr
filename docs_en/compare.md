# Compare datasets

Pick up to four datasets to see their period, resolution, update status, key DOIs and successors in one table.
Type a variable such as "precipitation", "2 m temperature" or `tp` into **Find a variable** to list **every dataset that has it** — no selection needed — with its name, units and whether it is **instantaneous, mean or accumulated**. Different short names for the same quantity (`tp`, `PRECTOT`, `prate` …) are grouped by CF standard name (see [Standard variables](variables.md)).
