EarthCARE Level-3 Global Gridded Product (L3_GRD) Now Available!
JAXA Earth Observation Research Center (EORC) is pleased to announce the release of EarhtCARE Level-3 Global Gridded Product (L3_GRD) on 13 October 2026. L3_GRD provides monthly global gridded statistics derived from EarthCARE Level-2 products and is suitable for climate, meteorological, and Earth system studies.

[Quick Report] EarthCARE Captured the Eye of Typhoon Sinlaku
The EarthCARE satellite (Japanese name: Hakuryu) successfully passed directly over the eye of Typhoon Sinlaku (Typhoon No.4 in 2026) on 13 April 2026, capturing unprecedented observations of the typhoon’s internal cloud structure.
