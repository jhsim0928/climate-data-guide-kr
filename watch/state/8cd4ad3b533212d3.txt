Temporal Extension, Updated Dataset
Release date:
Nov 2025
Product change:
The multi year interim dataset will be merged into corresponding multi year dataset.
Technical details:
The dataset cmems_mod_glo_phy_myint_0.083deg_P1D-m and cmems_mod_glo_phy_myint_0.083deg_P1M-m will be retired.
The data in these dataset will be put in the dataset cmems_mod_glo_phy_my_0.083deg_P1D-m and cmems_mod_glo_phy_my_0.083deg_P1M-m.
The official end of multiyear timeseries is 31/06/2021. After 01/07/2021, the data are from interim production.
