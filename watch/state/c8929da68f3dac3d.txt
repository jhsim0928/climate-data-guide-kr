New AI-corrected Sea Ice Thickness variable
Quality Improvement, Updated File Format
Release date:
Jun 2025
Product change:
The TOPAZ4 reanalysis assimilates sea ice thickness since the beginning of the CryoSAT-2 and SMOS satellites lifetime in 2010. The resulting discontinuity is corrected by an AI technique (see Edel et al. 2025 for details) in an additional variable that corrects the thin bias pre-2010 and therefore has a correct decreasing trend of sea ice volume.
Technical details:
The additional variable is called sithick_corr and will be equal to the model-based sithick after the year 2010.
