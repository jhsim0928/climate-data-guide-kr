Global Low Resolution Sea-Ice Drift Interim Climate Data Record, release 1
Demonstrational
Climate product
An Interim Climate Data Record of the sea-ice drift variable, associated to the Climate Data Record OSI-455. Based on Passive Microwave Radiometer data (SSMIS and AMSR2). Summer seasons are gap-filled using a Free Drift model forced with ERA5 10m wind data.
L3+L4 daily files.
