September 8, 2026: Indonesia fire situation
Indonesian fires intensify during El Niño events because prolonged drought in combination with drainage of peatlands
and land use change makes the landscape highly flammable. The graph below is updated daily and helps putting
the emissions of this year's El Niño in context of previous years. More background information and an even longer
data record can be found in this
CarbonBrief
piece. Province-level data can be found in our
emissions tool
Top graph shows annual emissions, bottom four the cumulative emissions for all of Indonesia and the
islands of Sumatra, Kalimantan, and Papua (Western New Guinea). While emissions in most regions dwarf those of neighboring
Papua New Guinea
emissions there exceed those seen in previous El Niño's, including 1997/1998.
September 4, 2026: New Website
We have redesigned the GFED website to provide users with an improved and easy‑to‑use tool for placing the
current fire situation into the context of previous years. Please visit the
current fire situation page
, where you can explore data at the GFED region level,
country level, continental level, and—where available—province or state level for selected countries.
The underlying text files and higher‑quality PDF figures are also available for download.
