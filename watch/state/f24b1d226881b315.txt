notice type: informational
NOAA ERSSTv6
is the new official sea surface temperature (SST) dataset used by NOAA to determine the current ENSO phase. All official NOAA products needing a long dataset have transitioned to this dataset. The
Relative Oceanic Niño Index
(RONI)—the official NOAA product used for monitoring and prediction of ENSO—has also been updated to use NOAA ERSSTv6.
notice type: enhancement
PSL OLR MJO Index
(OMI) used to study MJO events has been updated to near present and now uses the NOAA daily blended OLR.
notice type: enhancement
Relative Oceanic Niño Index
(RONI) - the official NOAA ENSO index replacing the ONI - is now available at PSL. It is similar to the
ONI but the tropical mean SSTs are subtracted out, reducing the impact
of the SST trend on the index. It will be updated monthly.
notice type: warning
NOAA ERSSTv5 dataset
will be discontinued by NOAA/NCEI in June 2026. It is being replaced by the NOAA ERSSTv6 dataset, which is
now available
on the PSL website.
Learn more and get ERSSTv6 updates from NCEI
