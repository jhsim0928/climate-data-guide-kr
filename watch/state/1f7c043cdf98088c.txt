November 20, 2026
New data set release: MEaSUREs Precipitation Phase and Rate from ATMS Passive Microwave Observations and Earth System Models using Transfer Learning, Version 1
MEaSUREs Precipitation Phase and Rate from ATMS Passive Microwave Observations and Earth System Models using Transfer Learning, Version 1 is now available at the NASA National Snow and Ice Data Center Distributed Active Archive Center (NSIDC DAAC). This data set, part of the NASA Making Earth System Data Records for Use in Research Environments (MEaSUREs) Program, contains estimates of precipitation phase (rain, snow, or clear) and total precipitation rate at approximately 15 km × 15 km resolution along the Suomi National Polar-orbiting Partnership (NPP) Advanced Technology Microwave Sounder (ATMS) swath, with a temporal coverage of 1 January 2012 to 29 December 2023.

November 19, 2026
New versions released: Moderate Resolution Imaging Spectroradiometer (MODIS), Version 7
Version 7 (Collection 7) of the Moderate Resolution Imaging Spectroradiometer (MODIS) data sets are now available at the NASA National Snow and Ice Data Center Distributed Active Archive Center (NSIDC DAAC). This data collection includes a suite of snow cover and sea ice data products derived from NASA’s Moderate Resolution Imaging Spectroradiometer (MODIS) instruments aboard both the Terra and Aqua satellites. MODIS Version 7 snow and sea ice products are derived using the same science algorithm used in MODIS Version 61 reprocessing.

The QuikSCAT/SeaWinds Twice-Daily SIR-Enhanced EASE-Grid 2.0 Radar Backscatter, Version 1 data set is now available at the NASA National Snow and Ice Data Center Distributed Active Archive Center (NSIDC DAAC). This data set provides Ku-band radar backscatter measurements, Sigma Nought (σ⁰, Sigma0), collected by the SeaWinds scatterometer instruments aboard the QuikSCAT and ADEOS-2 satellites. The SnowModel Outputs Over Northern Alaska Boreal Forest Snowpack, 2009–2023, Version 1 data set is now available at the NASA National Snow and Ice Data Center Distributed Active Archive Center (NSIDC DAAC).
