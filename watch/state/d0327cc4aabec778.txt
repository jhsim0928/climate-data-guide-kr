Simple Ocean Data Assimilation (SODA)
A global reanalysis that provides a history of the changing ocean/sea ice system at 5dy intervals beginning January 1980
Overview
The National Science Foundation funded Simple Ocean Data Assimilation (SODA) project aims to reconstruct the historical physical, and eventually biogeochemical, history of the global ocean/sea ice system with an architecture based on community-standard codes and data-sets. The project began as a research project at University of Maryland as an ocean/sea ice counterpart to, and exploiter of, the early atmospheric reanalyses and has evolved as the atmospheric reanalyses have developed..
Below are links to two versions: the ‘eddy-permitting’ SODA3 (version SODA3.15.2) and the higher resolution ‘eddy-resolving’ SODA4 (version SODA4.15.2). The two reanalyses have similar numerical architecture and constraining data sets. SODA3 is easier to work with since the file sizes are much smaller. SODA4 is suited for studies of coastal environments, ocean passages, and for any problems that need higher horizontal or vertical resolution or more realistic representation of the impact of flow instabilities.
