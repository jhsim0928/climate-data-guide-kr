ECMWF's Public Dataset Service has been providing access to a number of datasets. With the need to consolidate our data-provision infrastructure, this service is being phased out.
For most datasets listed below, the
Public Datasets Service
stopped on 1 June 2023.
For TIGGE and S2S the service was stopped on 27 May 2026.
The access method for the S2S and TIGGE datasets was migrated to
the new
ECMWF Data Store (ECDS)
opened on Tuesday, 9 UTC, 21 April 2026.
For more information how to use the ECDS, please refer to
Using the ECMWF data store - ECDS
Instructions how to change smoothly from the old WEB-API to the new CDS-API to access the data are available in
User guide for migration of S2S and TIGGE to ECDS
Whenever possible please switch to the alternative datasets listed in the table below.
Should datasets become available
elsewhere, this page will be updated.
For any q
uestions please contact us via the
ECMWF Support Portal
