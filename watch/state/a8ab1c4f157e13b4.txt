Related Datasets
GFED4
GFED4s was released in 2017 and described in
van der Werf et al. (2017)
It was the first GFED version to include small‑fire burned area, using the statistical approach introduced in
Randerson et al. (2012)
After 2016, emissions are no longer based on burned area due to changes in MODIS burned‑area collections.
GFED4s used Collection 5.1, which was superseded by Collection 6. Emissions for the post‑2016 period are derived
from MODIS active‑fire detections and a grid‑cell‑specific conversion calibrated using the 2003–2016 overlap period.
GFED4s covers 1997–2025 at 0.25° spatial resolution. We recommend using GFED5 when possible.
GFED 500‑meter
Although GFED spatial resolution has increased over successive versions, it remains coarser (0.25°) than the
satellite data used (mostly 500 m). To better align model and data resolution, a simplified GFED model was run
at 500‑meter resolution for the 2002 onward period, described in
Van Wees et al. (2022)
GFED5 is not provided at 500‑meter resolution because the small‑fire burned‑area algorithm requires spatial aggregation.
GFED500m offers greater spatial detail, whereas GFED5 includes small‑fire burned area and is available in near real time.
Historic Emissions (BB4CMIP)
Fire emissions prior to the satellite era are uncertain, but proxy data and modeling help characterize variability
and trends. The BB4CMIP dataset, described in
Van Marle et al. (2017)
was constructed for CMIP6 using GFED4s as an anchor dataset.
Ongoing efforts aim to harmonize BB4CMIP with GFED5 and update the dataset.
