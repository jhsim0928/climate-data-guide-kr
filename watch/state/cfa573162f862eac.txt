Dear Joao,
The Land Cover classification maps are normally released on an annual basis with a one-year delay, as described in the documentation. However, due to a contractual transition between project phases, the release schedule has been temporarily delayed.
The upcoming release timeline is as follows:
LC maps for 2023 and 2024: scheduled for release in end of March 2026
LC map for 2025: expected by end of end of February 2027
LC map for 2026: expected by end of February 2028

Dear John,
Thanks for your message.
We have recently updated the release plan. The land cover maps for 2023, 2024 and 2025 will now be released together to ECMWF in June this year.
The subsequent timeline for review and public release will depend on the standard review process.
This represents a slight delay of around 3 months for the 2023–2024 products compared to the previous schedule but it also brings the 2025 product forward by approximately eight months.
The aim is to move towards a more timely, near-real-time release cycle.
We will share further updates as the release progresses.
Kind regards,
Celine
