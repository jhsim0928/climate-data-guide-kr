Operational Products
The list gives a summary of the available “operational” products, which are continuously generated close to real-time in support to climate monitoring. Links to the most recent product family for the respective parameter with direct links to the data ordering are given in the column “availability” for the currently generated Interim Climate Data Records (ICDR).
Information on earlier versions, which have been discontinued (status “DC”) are available on demand only. For information on these products please refer to the
Change Log
-section of this webpage.
