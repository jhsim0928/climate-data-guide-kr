Outgoing Longwave Radiation - Daily CDR
Configuration Item ID: 01B-21
The daily Outgoing Longwave Radiation (OLR) Climate Data Record (CDR) measures the amount of terrestrial radiation released into space and, by extension, the amount of cloud cover and water vapor that intercepts that radiation in the atmosphere.
The final record is generated through a combination of statistical techniques, including OLR regression, instrument ambient temperature prediction coefficients, and inter-satellite bias corrections. The daily OLR period of record is 1979–present.
