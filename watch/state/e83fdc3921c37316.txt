Global Dynamic Land Cover
View in the data viewer
Global land monitoring is an indispensable tool in the pursuit of sustainable development. By tracking changes in land cover and land use, we can begin to understand the forces behind them. While many of the most powerful forces shaping our land—such as urbanization, agricultural intensification, and climate change—present serious societal and environmental challenges, they also offer opportunities for innovation and adaptation. Assisted by a clear picture of these dynamics, scientists, policy makers, and resource managers are empowered to address these challenges head on.
The Global Dynamic Land Cover product, with specific regard to the 100 m datasets covering the years 2015-2019, offers annual global land cover maps and cover fraction layers, providing a detailed view of land cover at three classification levels. It uses modern data analysis techniques to ensure temporal consistency and accuracy, with the latest version achieving 80% accuracy at class level 1 on each continent. The product also includes continuous field layers, or "fraction maps", that provide proportional estimates for vegetation and ground cover for the land cover types. These features make it a versatile tool for a wide range of applications, including forest monitoring, rangeland management, crop monitoring, biodiversity conservation, climate modelling, and urban planning.
Built upon the Global Dynamic Land Cover dataset, the Copernicus Global Land Cover and Tropical Forest Mapping and Monitoring Service (LCFM) not only extends the time series of the 100 m Copernicus Global Land Cover layers (2015–2019), but also enhances the existing service by delivering a high-resolution (10 m), dynamic global land cover service (2020–2026). The 10 m Global Land Cover Map (LCM) for 2020 and the 10 m Pan-Tropical Tree Cover Density (TCD) for 2020 are already available.
