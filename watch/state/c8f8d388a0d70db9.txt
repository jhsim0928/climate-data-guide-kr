DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE
Cessation of Suomi National Polar-orbiting Partnership (S-NPP) data to users on/after November 2, 2026
August 3, 2026
Notice of Changes
Office of Satellite and Product Operations
Topic
: Cessation of Suomi National Polar-orbiting Partnership (S-NPP) data to users on/after November 2, 2026
Date/Time Issued
: August 3, 2026 at 1600 UTC
Product(s) or Data Impacted
: All S-NPP data products
Date/Time of Initial Impact
: November 2, 2026 at 1300 UTC
(8:00 AM EST)
Date/Time of Expected End
: N/A
Length of Outage
: Permanent
Details/Specifics:
NOAA will end delivery of all remaining science products from the Suomi National Polar-Orbiting Partnership (S-NPP) satellite at 13:00 UTC (8:00 AM EST) on November 2, 2026
ATMS - data will no longer be available after November 2, 2026 at 13:00 UTC (8:00 AM EST)
CERES - data will no longer be available after November 2, 2026, at 13:00 UTC (8:00 AM EST)
OMPS - Nadir - data will no longer be available after November 2, 2026, at 13:00 UTC (8:00 AM EST)
OMPS - Limb- data will no longer be available after November 2, 2026, at 13:00 UTC (8:00 AM EST)
VIIRS - data will no longer be available after November 2, 2026, at 13:00 UTC (8:00 AM EST)
HRD - Direct Broadcast will be disabled after November 2, 2026, at 13:00 UTC (8:00 AM EST)
CrIS - End of Operational Use occurred on December 23, 2025
Further updates regarding S-NPP will be provided as the November 2, 2026 date approaches.
All users should transition to using NOAA-21 (Primary) and NOAA-20 (Secondary) science products, which are accessible via NOAA Product Distribution and Access (PDA). NOAA-21 and NOAA-20 provide complementary science data via similar instruments.
