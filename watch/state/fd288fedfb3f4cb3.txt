Data Assimilation
Numerical weather prediction centres use data assimilation to create the initial conditions needed to run their models and produce weather forecasts. Preparations are underway to include EarthCARE observations in the European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts (ECMWF) data assimilation system.
FIND OUT MORE
Announcements of opportunity
Announcements of opportunity are occasionally opened relating to EarthCARE, for example, for new groups to participate in EarthCARE Cal/Val activities and gain early access to EarthCARE data products.
FIND OUT MORE
Calibration/Validation
Calibration activities ensure that the EarthCARE instruments are performing well and to their established baselines. Validation activities ensure that the EarthCARE data and products are meaningful and well characterised with respect to uncertainties due to sensitivity or resolution.
