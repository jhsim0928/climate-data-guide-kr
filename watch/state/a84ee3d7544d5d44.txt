[MDSOP-377] Application of retention periods to ARCO datacubes
On Going
2026-04-27 00:00 UTC - On going
In the coming weeks, we will apply retention periods to some ARCO datacubes, in line with the retention rules declared in the Product User Manuals (PUMs).
A retention period defines how long data remains accessible through ARCO-based services before older data is no longer exposed. As a consequence, users may notice changes in the start date or temporal extent of some time series.
This maintenance concerns ARCO-based services only, including:
Subset GUI
Copernicus Marine Toolbox subset
MyOcean Viewer
Original files are not affected by this maintenance. This means that the File Browser and `copernicusmarine get` are not impacted.
If you notice an unexpected change in temporal availability through ARCO-based services, please contact Copernicus Marine User Support and provide the product ID, dataset ID, service used, and requested time range.
If you have any question, please contact

COPERNICUS MARINE NEW SERVICE RELEASE ON 7 JULY 2026
Improvement
Copernicus Marine Service is pleased to deliver New Service Release on 7 July 2026.
The main characteristics of this release will be :
BLUE OCEAN
SST_BS_SST_L4_NRT_OBSERVATIONS_010_006
SST_MED_SST_L4_NRT_OBSERVATIONS_010_004
: New climatology will be constructed over the period 1991–2020 to replace the current climatology based on the period 1985-2005. This evolution also implies the reprocessing of all the anomaly data records for both Mediterranean Sea and Black Sea for Near Real Time products from 2008 to present.
WAVE_GLO_PHY_SPC-FWK_L3_NRT_014_002
: A new dataset is created due to the integration of Sentinel-1C measurements in Level-3 NRT Fireworks products.
WAVE_GLO_PHY_SWH_L3_MY_014_005
: The last version of Multi Year Significant Wave Heigh Level 3 based on CCi Sea State Project will replace the previous version of the product. A new dataset will be created, and the old datasets will be retired.
WAVE_GLO_PHY_SWH_L4_NRT_014_003
: Increased spatial (from 2° to 0.5°) and temporal (from daily to 6 hours) resolution of the instantaneous (MIOST mapping approach) dataset of the product.
WAVE_GLO_PHY_SWH_L3_NRT_014_001
: The temporal coverage of the ten 1 Hz Significant Wave Height datasets (_PT1S) is reduced to the last two years of available data. Earlier records are now available through the updated
WAVE_GLO_PHY_SWH_L3_MY_014_005
product.
WIND_GLO_PHY_L3_NRT_012_002
: New FY-3E WindRAD L3 Near Real Time datasets at two horizontal resolutions will be added to the product.
MULTIOBS_GLO_PHY_S_SURFACE_MYNRT_015_013
: Updated upstream Sea Surface Salinity (SSS) input data. The product now uses the
MULTIOBS_GLO_PHY_SSS_L3_MYNRT_015_014
Level-3 SMOS Sea Surface Salinity dataset, improving the robustness and consistency of the processing chain.
MULTIOBS_GLO_PHY_SSS_L4_MY_015_015
: Use the "Centre Aval de Traitement des Données du Satellite (CATDS)" L3G products (Gaussian temporal average of Sea Surface Salinity, SSS) instead of the CATDS L3Q, simple average of SSS for the Soil Moisture and Ocean Salinity products.
SEALEVEL_EUR_PHY_L3_NRT_008_059
SEALEVEL_GLO_PHY_L3_NRT_008_044
: New datasets are being added to the catalog in preparation for the future integration of Sentinel-6B into the DUACS system. These datasets will remain empty for an indefinite period. Operational production of these data is scheduled to begin in late 2026, after the mission's commissioning phase.
GREEN OCEAN
GLOBAL_MULTIYEAR_BGC_001_033
: The reanalysis, which provides an estimate of low and mid-trophic levels for the period 1998–2024, is extended to the first half of 2025.
WHITE OCEAN
NWATL_ANALYSISFORECAST_PHY_ICE_017_001
: The COLAB-MFC starts with a new near-real-time product which is based on the Coastal Ice Ocean Prediction System for the East Coast of Canada developed by Environment and Climate Change Canada and distributed by the Meteorological Service of Canada. The new product covers the Northwestern Atlantic along the North American coastline and provides 48-hour forecasts in hourly files, issued four times a day at 1/36° resolution and 99 vertical z-levels. It includes both ocean physics and sea ice variables.
