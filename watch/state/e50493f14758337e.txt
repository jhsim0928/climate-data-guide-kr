Introduction of GLOPHYTS algorithm, new carbon dataset, new gradient variable to CHL, and variables to 300m PFT.
New Dataset, Quality Improvement, Updated Dataset, Updated File Format
Release date:
Nov 2026
Product change:
- Extension of the gradient variable to the multi-sensor daily L3 plankton datasets. Previously these were only produced in OLCI plankton datasets.
- Integration of the OCROC algorithms from LOG into ACRI-ST's processing chain in GLO products. The new POC and DOC variables will be integrated in newly created carbon (""car"") datasets. POC algorithm results in a combination of different algorithms in function of the optical water classes. The DOC algorithm approach consider a temporal window to account for the fate of the organic matter with neural network.
- Update and improve of the phytoplankton functional type (PFT) time series for the OLCI era by integration of the GLOPHYTS algorithm into ACRI-ST's processing chain. The improvement of the PFT product is based on a recalibration of the OLCI product allowing to get a consistent time-series over the ocean colour archive.
- PFT variables will be added to the existing olci-300m-plankton datasets, the method is adjusted from our existing PFT algorithms.
Technical details:
Update of the scripts for handling data is needed.
Complementary information:
A double-dissemination period is planned from November 3th 2026 to Janvier 12th, 2027. See
https://help.marine.copernicus.eu/en/articles/6823705-copernicus-marine-service-releases-double-distribution-process
for more information.
