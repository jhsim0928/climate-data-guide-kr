Danish Meteorological Institute now responsible for the operational production of this dataset
Release date:
Jun 2025
Product change:
As part of a collaboration agreement established in 2023 between the Copernicus Climate Change Service (C3S) and the Copernicus Marine Service, the SST-TAC has assumed responsibility for the production of SST Essential Climate Variables (ECVs) for C3S, in full compliance with C3S specifications and guidelines. Consequently, this product is brokered from the C3S catalogue and made available through the Copernicus Marine Service.
The Danish Meteorological Institute (DMI) is now responsible for the operational production of this dataset, which spans from 1982 to the present and provides daily, gap-free global fields of both sea surface temperature (SST) and sea-ice surface temperature (IST). The product is generated using the DMI Optimal Interpolation (DMIOI) system and is based on Level 3U single-sensor input data from the ESA CCI SST v3.0 dataset for the SST component, and on AASTI v2.1 together with the C3S interim Climate Data Record (iCDR) for the IST component.

Release date:
Nov 2024
Product change:
For the Global Reprocessed SST product, a change in Production Unit, production system and upstream input data result in a new global L4, gap-free daily product at 0.05 degrees resolution from 1982 onward, combining sea surface (SST) and sea-ice surface (IST) temperatures.
The new PU is the Danish Meteorological Institute (DMI) and using the DMI Optimal Interpolation (DMIOI) system, is releasing the first global combined SST/IST L4, gap-free, product using as input data the L3U single sensor products of the ESA CCI SST v3.0 dataset for the SST component and the AASTI v2.1 and C3S iCDR for the IST component of the product.
