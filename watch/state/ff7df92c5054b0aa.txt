Aura Stories
Aura (Latin for breeze) obtains measurements of ozone, aerosols and key gases throughout the atmosphere.
Type
Orbiter
Launch
July 15, 2004
Target
Earth
Objective
Measure trace gases in Earth's atmosphere
System Analysis
Potential Evolution of the Aura Mission
The Aura satellite and its instruments are in good/excellent health, having aged well. This status is expected to continue to the end of the mission. Insufficient power generation by the solar array is conservatively predicted to be the life-limiting factor for the Aura satellite and instruments by mid-2028.
