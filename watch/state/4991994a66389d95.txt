Reprocessed L4 with enhanced MIOST technique
New Dataset, Quality Improvement
Release date:
Nov 2025
Product change:
A new MIOST version, considering equatorial waves and barotrope high frequency sea level variations, allows us to better resolves the mesoscale signal with nearly 3% error reduction at mesoscale in the equatorial band as presented in (Ballarotta et al., 2023). We propose here to use this MIOST version, enhanced with additional modes, to reprocess the 30y of L4 products for Global and Europe area.
This production is proposed a demontrative production that will not be extended in time. It may be used for a future L4 official production. In the meanwhile, all user feedback is welcome to identif strengths and weaknesses of this mapping.
Technical details:
This new L4 production will be diseminated in a new dataset :
SEALEVEL_GLO_PHY_L4_MY_008_047 / cmems_obs-sl_glo_phy-ssh_my_allsat-demo-l4-duacs-0.125deg_P1D-i
"demo" identifies the demonstrative status of this production that will not be extented in time. All user feedback are welcome. They will help validate or identify the weaknesses of this mapping, which may be used in a future version of the official products.

GLOBAL OCEAN GRIDDED L4 SEA SURFACE HEIGHTS AND DERIVED VARIABLES REPROCESSED (1993-ONGOING)
Release date:
Nov 2023
Product change:
The production of MY time series will evolve to take into account a change in altimetry standards. Several standards/corrections applied will evolve. The main evolutions will be : *
a change of ocean tide : FES2022 instead of FES2014 model
a change of the MSS : hybrid SIO2022/CNES_CLS2022 instead of hybrid SIO/CNES_CLS2015/DT15)
These changes will improve the quality of the products, in particular on mesoscale signals and in coastal areas. The global and regional sea level biases, induced by this change, will be managed during L3/L4 processing in order to ensure a seamless transition for users and maintain the quality of the products at climatic scales.
Technical details:
The following "_myint" datasets will be defined to store this production and differentiate it from the DT-2021 series already available. The previous “*my*_” datasets remain available but will no longer be maintained. A full reprocessed series, using the new standards/corrections should be available end 2024.
